------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[B] Palette and style
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Iron and durasteel - the iron ramp (353535 to e5e5e5) and the bluish
durasteel ramp (525660 to e9eaec) were sampled from futureazoo's iron and
durasteel machine sets. Used in block/laser_turret_base.png (durasteel
plate).

Cable - the rubber and copper colours of block/electric_conduit.png come
from futureazoo's insulated_cable_copper.
